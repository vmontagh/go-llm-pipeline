## Security Policy

We take the security of `go-llm-pipeline` seriously and appreciate responsible
disclosure of vulnerabilities.

### Reporting a vulnerability

Please **do not** open a public issue for security vulnerabilities.

Instead, report privately using GitHub's
[private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability):

1. Open a [new private security advisory](https://github.com/vmontagh/go-llm-pipeline/security/advisories/new)
   (or go to the **Security** tab and click **Report a vulnerability**).
2. Include a clear description, affected versions, reproduction steps, and any
   proof-of-concept or suggested remediation.

Published fixes are listed under
[security advisories](https://github.com/vmontagh/go-llm-pipeline/security/advisories).

If you cannot use private vulnerability reporting, open a minimal issue asking a
maintainer for a private channel, without disclosing the vulnerability details
publicly.

### Response expectations

- We aim to acknowledge new reports within **3 business days**.
- We will provide an assessment and remediation plan within **10 business days**
  of acknowledgement, and keep you informed of progress until resolution.
- Once a fix is available, we will coordinate a disclosure timeline with you and
  credit your report unless you prefer to remain anonymous.

### Scope

Security reports are welcome for any code in this repository. Please report
issues in third-party dependencies to their respective maintainers, though we
are happy to help coordinate when the impact reaches this project.
